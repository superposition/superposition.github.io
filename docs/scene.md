# The station scene

Three layers share one canvas: `viewBox="0 0 2400 1500"`, horizon at y=860,
vanishing point at (1200, 860), transparent backgrounds, no external
references. Each layer is generated by a standard-library script and the SVG is
committed beside it, so the drawing and the code that made it cannot drift.
View a layer on `#000000`: it is line work for the site's black ground, not for
a white page.

## City and ground — `scripts/scene/city.py`

Draws the ground the action happens above. One pinhole camera, `CAM_H` 55 world
units above the plane, `FOCAL` 900px, vanishing point at the centre of the
horizon, so every edge in the drawing is a vertical, a horizontal, or a line
through that point and the whole frame shares one perspective. The city is kept
out of a corridor where `|x| = 0.42 · z`, which projects to a fixed ±378px from
the vanishing point: the middle of the frame stays open ground for the station,
its camera and the traffic, and the massing closes in on both sides. Depth is
carried by value and line weight, never hue — near masses are 4px cream
outlines, the middle distance 2px dust, the far city a single 1px dust line —
and by black fills that make a nearer mass occlude what stands behind it. Amber
appears only as 1px lit-window marks, floodlight cans, the searchlight rays of
the lit masts, and a handful of glints on the apron; cyan marks two antennae;
red marks two beacons on the tallest towers and nothing else.

**Primitives, all in world coordinates unless noted:**

- `block(xs0, xs1, z0, z1, top_y)` — the unit of the city. A front face at `z0`,
  a roof running back to `z1`, and the one side face that faces the vanishing
  point, black-filled and stroked on its silhouette and its corners. `simple`
  drops to the four lines a 1px far block can carry.
- `window_rows(...)` — one dashed row per storey, 1px amber, the dash pattern
  jittered per block so no two facades light alike; floor height 2.6 and window
  pitch 1.9 world units, so the marks shrink with depth and disappear past the
  point where they would be smaller than a pixel.
- `roof_detail(...)` — a parapet plus one of a mast with crossbars, a plant
  room, a dish, or a water tank.
- `pad(...)` — the assembly hardstand: outline, inner edge, radial and cross
  joints, a tie-down grid, a pad circle with crosshair, corner marks, and
  hazard ticks along the front edge.
- `lattice_tower(...)`, `gantry_truss(...)` — the assembly gantry: two braced
  service towers with hammerheads and aerials, a trussed beam between them, a
  trolley and a hanging hoist.
- `flood_mast(...)` — stem, base flare, head with four cans; a lit mast's cans
  are amber and three searchlight rays leave the head.
- `tank(...)`, `blast_wall(...)`, `rails(...)` — storage cylinders with banding,
  low walls in perspective, and track pairs with ties spaced in world units so
  they compress with depth.
- `ground_plane(...)` — the horizon rule, ten avenues converging on the
  vanishing point, and rungs at an even pitch *in the picture* (bunched world
  spacing turns the last pixels above the horizon into a grey band).
- `weather(...)` — seeded small marks: 1px haze in the horizon band, dust motes
  over the lower frame, and a few amber glints on the slate. Nothing moving,
  nobody walking.

**Parameters.** `--seed` (the only source of variation), `--rows` (depth rows
of city, default 11), `--grid` (block width and street gap multiplier), `--towers`
(landmark towers among the mid blocks), `--windows` (window density
multiplier), `--floodlights` and `--lit` (masts, and how many of them are lit),
`--weather` (mark density), `--height` (block height multiplier), `--density`
(rooftop detail). Tier geometry — near `z < 230`, mid `z < 830`, far beyond —
lives in `TIER` with each tier's block width, gap, height band and how far from
the vanishing point it may start.

    python scripts/scene/city.py --seed 1981 --out assets/scene/city.svg

Roughly 88 KB, 5.3% of the frame in ink, identical bytes on a re-run with the
same seed.
